# Vendor control framework v1

## IAM-1 workforce access

Vendor must require phishing-resistant MFA for privileged access, review access
at least quarterly, revoke access promptly, and provide dated evidence. Policy
statement alone is partial evidence; configuration or audit export is stronger.

## ENC-1 encryption

Customer data must use TLS 1.2 or newer in transit and reviewed encryption at
rest. Key ownership, rotation, and exception scope must be documented.

## IR-1 incident response

Vendor must maintain tested incident response plan and notify customer under
contractual deadline. Undated plan without exercise record is partial.

## SUB-1 subprocessors

Vendor must disclose subprocessors, processing purpose, location, and change
notification. Missing location or purpose is gap.

Model output is evidence-only risk handoff. This sample cannot accept vendor,
grant exception, procure, contract, onboard, or invoke downstream workflow.
